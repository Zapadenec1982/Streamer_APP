ModESP Streamer is the remote for the ModESP Streamer, a network streamer with its own touch screen that plays Bluetooth, AirPlay, internet radio, a memory card, media servers and sound from a computer over USB.

The app shows what plays and puts the everyday controls in your hand:

• Player: the cover, the title and the artist, where the sound comes from and in what format. Play, pause and skip as far as the source allows. The volume, with the phone's own volume buttons too.
• Sources: every source the streamer has, with what it would play now. A tap switches.
• Radio: the station between ‹ and ›, six preset buttons (a tap listens, a hold stores what plays), your station list to arrange, and the Radio Browser catalogue by country, genre or name.
• Music: the memory card's library and the media servers on your network, with seeking, repeat and shuffle, and a mini-player on every page.
• Sound: the streamer's sound presets, Bass and Treble, loudness, the maximum and the start-up volume, and the night leveler that keeps quiet and loud passages close together, by the clock if you like.
• Equalizer: the curve with a point to drag for each band, the bands one by one, your own sound, and filters in and out as text (REW, Equalizer APO).
• Speakers: the setup, the port frequency found by the streamer's microphone or by ear, the balance and the fine tuning.
• The streamer itself: its screen, language and clock, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth pairing, AirPlay and DLNA, its login, firmware updates and backups.
• Several streamers: found on your Wi-Fi by themselves or added by address, and remembered.

The app speaks English, Ukrainian, German and Polish, wears the streamer's colour scheme, and works with TalkBack and large text.

No streamer yet? Open the demo on the first screen: a made-up streamer with its own tracks and stations shows everything the app does.

What it needs: a ModESP Streamer on the same Wi-Fi network as the phone. The app talks only to that streamer, on your home network. No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking.
