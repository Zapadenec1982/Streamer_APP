# ModESP Streamer app: privacy policy

The ModESP Streamer app is the remote for a ModESP Streamer network player. It is made by the
author of [ModESP_Streamer](https://github.com/Zapadenec1982/ModESP_Streamer).

**What leaves the phone.** The app talks only to ModESP Streamer panels on your local network. It
reads a panel's state and sends the panel your commands (volume, source, station, sound) over HTTP
and WebSocket. Nothing is sent to the author or to anyone else: the app has no server, no account,
no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

**Finding panels.** The app looks for panels on your Wi-Fi with mDNS (Bonjour). This stays on your
local network.

**What the app keeps on the phone.** The panels you added (their names and addresses), which one is
chosen, and the app's language. If a panel asks for its login, the login is kept in the phone's
encrypted storage and is sent only to that panel. Covers and station lists come from your panel and
are held only while the app runs.

**Links.** The panel's own settings open in your browser, at your panel's address on your network.

**Permissions.** Network access, to reach your panel, and Wi-Fi multicast, to find panels on your
network. The app does not use the microphone, the camera, location, contacts or your files.

**Removing.** «Forget» removes a panel and its login from the app. Uninstalling the app removes
everything it kept.

**Children.** The app is not directed to children.

**Changes.** When the app's handling of data changes, this page changes with it.

**Contact.** The developer address on the app's Google Play page.
